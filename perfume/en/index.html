Read my full story

I was once a full-time mother.
Every day I was a mother, a wife, a daughter.
I cared for everyone — yet in the mirror, I no longer recognised myself.
“Who am I?” I couldn’t find the answer.

What stayed with me through it all was scent.
When I was tired, a drop of essential oil cleared my mind;
when life was chaotic, a breath of fragrance steadied me.
It was the one small moment I kept for myself.

Two years ago, I met TimeWaver.
I began using it to find the scents that were “in tune” with me.
Scent, I realised, is more than something pleasant —
the right scent made me more present, more passionate, and gave me back my drive.

Then the people around me began to change.
The first thing they said when they saw me was often, “Wow, you smell lovely!”
More smiles, more acceptance, more love.
Even my children wanted to come closer and listen.

I understood then:
scent cannot be seen or touched, yet it reaches the heart first.
When someone smells lovely, you naturally step a little closer and stay a little longer.
When your scent is right, good people and good things find their way to you.

So I blended my first perfume for myself — I called it “The Present”.
Each time I wear it, I return to who I really am. At ease, and whole.

Now, I would like to hand this moment to you.

— Julia Yuen

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How is Scent of Now different from a DIY perfume workshop?

At a DIY workshop you choose scents by personal taste. With Scent of Now, Julia first learns your intention, then uses TimeWaver information field analysis to match the scent that suits you right now. Everything is done remotely — no visit needed.

Do we need to meet in person?

No. After payment you complete an intention questionnaire; Julia does the analysis and blending remotely and ships the perfume to you.

How long until I receive my perfume?

It ships within 7–10 working days after payment and questionnaire submission.

Do you ship overseas?

Yes. Local Hong Kong shipping is free; overseas shipping is quoted by region — please WhatsApp us first.

What ingredients do you use?

Natural plant essential oils are the main source of fragrance, hand-blended to each client’s formula.

Can I order it as a gift?

Yes. The recipient can fill in the intention questionnaire, or the giver can fill it in on their behalf. An intention card and formula certificate are included.

Can I reorder the same perfume?

Yes. Every formula is kept on file, so you can reorder the original blend or adjust it as your life changes.

What if I don’t like the scent?

Before blending, Julia asks which scents you like and dislike, and confirms the scent direction with you on WhatsApp. Blending only starts once you agree. Harmonise and Manifest also include a 30-day follow-up.

What is the named Signature label?

Every perfume has its own name in the format “Scent name · Your name-Signature”, for example Inner Radiance · Julia-Signature.

What is the 30-day follow-up?

After about 30 days of use, Julia checks in on WhatsApp about your experience and shares ways to use the scent in line with your intention. Manifest also includes one remote TimeWaver frequency adjustment — no need to send the perfume back.

Can I get a refund?

You can get a full refund before blending begins, once the scent direction has been confirmed. After blending starts, the perfume is a personalised item and cannot be returned or exchanged. If it is damaged in transit, we will send a replacement free of charge.

Is it suitable for teenagers?

It is suitable for ages 12 and over, with the essential oil concentration lowered according to age. For under-12s, please WhatsApp us first.

Can a scent help me feel more emotionally balanced?

Scent is one of the senses most closely linked to mood. Many people find that wearing a scent that feels truly their own helps them slow down, breathe more deeply and feel more settled and focused on busy or tiring days. Scent of Now matches your blend to your intention, so it can become a small daily ritual of caring for yourself. Everyone experiences scent differently, so simply enjoy it in your own way.
