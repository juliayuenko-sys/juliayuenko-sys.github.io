常見問題FAQ

10ml 滾珠瓶要滴幾多滴精油？

以 2% 計約 4 滴，其餘加滿基底油。1% 約 2 滴，3% 約 6 滴。

稀釋比例點計？

精油滴數 = 容量 (ml) × 比例 % × 20。例如 30ml × 2% × 20 = 12 滴。

用咩做基底油？

常用有分餾椰子油、荷荷巴油、甜杏仁油。滾珠香水亦可用荷荷巴油，質地清爽又耐放。

幾款精油混合點分？

先計總滴數，再按你鍾意嘅比例分。最簡單係平均分；想某種香味突出，就俾佢多一兩滴。

How many drops of essential oil in a 10ml roller bottle?

About 4 drops at 2%, topped up with carrier oil. Around 2 drops at 1% and 6 drops at 3%.

How do you calculate essential oil dilution?

Drops = bottle size (ml) × dilution % × 20. For example, 30ml × 2% × 20 = 12 drops.

Which carrier oil should I use?

Fractionated coconut, jojoba and sweet almond oil are common choices. Jojoba works well for roll-on perfume because it's light and keeps well.

How do I split drops across several oils?

Work out the total first, then share it out. An even split is simplest; give one or two extra drops to the oil you want to stand out.
