常見問題FAQ

528Hz 係咩？

528Hz 係索菲亞頻率其中一個，常用喺冥想同放鬆時作背景音。科學研究有限，建議當作放鬆輔助。

雙耳節拍要戴耳筒嗎？

要。左右耳播放略有不同嘅頻率，例如 200Hz 同 210Hz，大腦會感受到 10Hz 嘅輕柔律動。冇耳筒就冇效果。

432Hz 同 440Hz 有咩分別？

440Hz 係 A 音嘅國際標準音高；432Hz 係稍低嘅另一種調音，有人覺得聽落較柔和。

聽幾耐好？

一般 10–20 分鐘已經足夠。以自己舒服為準，唔需要長時間播放。

What is 528 Hz?

It's one of the Solfeggio frequencies, often used as a calm background tone for meditation. Evidence for specific effects is limited, so think of it as a relaxation aid.

Do binaural beats need headphones?

Yes. Each ear hears a slightly different tone, such as 200 Hz and 210 Hz, and your brain perceives a gentle 10 Hz pulse. Speakers won't create the effect.

What's the difference between 432 Hz and 440 Hz?

440 Hz is the standard tuning pitch for the note A. 432 Hz is a slightly lower tuning that some listeners find softer.

How long should I listen?

10–20 minutes is plenty for most people. Go by what feels comfortable.
