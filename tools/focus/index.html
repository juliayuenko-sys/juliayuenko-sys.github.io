常見問題FAQ

番茄鐘溫習法係咩？

專心 25 分鐘，休息 5 分鐘，重複幾輪。時間短而清晰，較容易開始同堅持。

溫習聽咩音樂好？

冇歌詞、穩定嘅聲音最適合，例如雨聲、棕噪音或輕柔琴音。音量要細。

棕噪音、粉紅噪音有咩分別？

棕噪音低沉，好似瀑布或者大風；粉紅噪音較平均，好似穩定嘅雨聲。兩種都比白噪音柔和。

專注頻率係咩？

係雙耳節拍：左右耳播放略有不同嘅音，大腦感受到約 14Hz 嘅律動。要戴耳筒；效果因人而異。

What is the Pomodoro technique?

Focus for 25 minutes, break for 5, and repeat. Short, clear blocks make it easier to start and keep going.

What's the best background sound for studying?

Steady sounds without lyrics, like rain, brown noise or soft piano. Keep the volume low.

Brown noise vs pink noise?

Brown noise is a deep rumble, like a waterfall. Pink noise is more even, like steady rain. Both are softer than white noise.

What are focus beats?

Binaural beats: each ear hears a slightly different tone and your brain perceives a pulse of about 14 Hz. Use headphones. Effects vary from person to person.
